These remedies were not invented. They were remembered. They were passed down by people who had no pharmacy around the corner and no doctor on call, only the plants and foods around them and the wisdom of those who came before.

The ingredients are simple, and most are already in your kitchen. But be patient with them. Food-based remedies work the way the old ones always have: quietly and gently, a little every day.

Start with one or two recipes that speak to you. Make them part of your morning or evening. Then come back and try another. That is how healing habits are built.
